API keys

Keys are shown as their first and last 5 characters and are never revealed. To replace one, click the field and paste the new key (Enter or click away to save, Esc to cancel). A config/keys.json next to the app pre-fills empty fields; a key entered here overrides it, and Remove goes back to it.

Network

Where OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini or Deepgram are blocked (mainland China, Hong Kong…), route all of the app's API traffic through a proxy: the address of your VPN / proxy client (Clash, V2Ray, Shadowsocks… usually a local port). Leave empty to use the system proxy settings. Saves as you type and takes effect immediately.
